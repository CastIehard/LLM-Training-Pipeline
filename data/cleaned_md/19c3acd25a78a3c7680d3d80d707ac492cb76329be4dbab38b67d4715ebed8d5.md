**@supriyaghimire481**

You can change your visa after obtaining an offer letter for studienkolleg course from any of the universities. Then you can apply for visa change in Immigration Office with documents like your Offer Letter, Health Insurance, APS Certificate, Blocked Account for Euro 11208 for a year (Euro 934 per month), Academic and B2 Language Certificates, copy of Passport and Ausbildung Visa etc. Please enquire once in a week in immigration office after submitting your application and documents to know the updates of visa change.

# Central Student Advisory Service

## **Your Portal for Questions About Your Studies**

Do you have questions about degree programs or need guidance in choosing your field of study? Are you already enrolled at UTN and looking for support? The Central Student Advisory Service will be happy to assist you.

We offer advice to prospective and current students on all non-academic matters related to studies at UTN. There are many reasons to seek advice – from exploring study options and choosing a degree program at UTN to advice on how to best tackle challenges while you are studying. Our key areas of advising include:

Our advising services are **personal**, **confidential**, and **independent**. We look forward to supporting you on your individual journey – before, during, and after your studies.

Advising sessions are available in person (Ulmenstraße 52h, Floor 4, 90443 Nuremberg) on campus or online via Zoom.

## Services for Schools

UTN's Central Student Advisory Service is the first point of contact for schools that want to support their students in choosing a course of study. Whether you are interested in information events at your school, visits to the university, individual counseling formats, or digital offerings, we are happy to assist you.

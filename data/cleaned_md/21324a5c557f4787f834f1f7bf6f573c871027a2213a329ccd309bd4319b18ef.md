I tried posting earlier, but it disappeared, so I will try again. Just a heads up....it took 7 months to get into a psychiatrist in Germany, and another 3 months to get tested to get a prescription for meds from a German doctor. It can take awhile. I believe that you may need a prescription from a German doctor to get meds in Germany. When my daughter finally was able to see the psychiatrist and was able to get a prescription for meds, it was paid for by the public insurance system, which was a pleasant surprise.

My daughter tried to get meds in the USA, during summer break, but the psychiatrist wouldn't give her a prescription when he found out that she was permanently living in Germany. He said that he wasn't legally allowed to give her a prescription, even though she is American. So, be aware of this. I wouldn't want you to fall through the cracks and not get meds for 12 months.

**Study in Germany** Study in Germany

24.10.2025 International students: Requirements to study in Germany Germany is one of the most attractive countries for international students not only because of the variety of different educational programs but also because of the possibility to study in different languages!

13.05.20250 Cost & Search: Student accommodation in Germany The next step after receiving acceptance from a German university is a housing search. We have collected the most important and useful information about the possible types of housing in Germany, their advantages and disadvantages, costs and ways of searching for an accommodation.

05.03.2025 At public universities: Tuition fees in Germany In Germany education policy is the responsibility of the 16 federal states with not much influence of the German government. The Constitutional Court (_Bundesverfassungsgericht_) decided on 26th of january 2005 that the German government will not have the power to strictly prevent the federal states from establishing their own individual fees.

20.01.20250 International students: Your first steps in Germany Are you coming soon to study in Germany? We have collected the most important information about the first things which you should do at the beginning of the semester. We also recommend you start searching for your accommodation in Germany before your arrival.

15.01.2025 Education in Germany: How much does it cost? How much does it cost to live and study in Germany? It is the most important question for those who are planning to study in one of the higher education institutions in Germany. On this page, we have collected basic information to roughly represent the cost of living for a student in Germany.

11.06.2024 Study in Germany: How to apply to the university? Studying in Germany can be attractive to many international students! However, sometimes the admission process to some universities and programs can be too complicated. It is especially important to inform yourself in advance about the possible difficulties and nuances when applying. And we will help with this!

15.05.2024 Education in Germany: How to finance yourself? If you have chosen one of the German universities, you should think in advance about the possibility to finance your education in Germany. Fortunately, there are many different ways to do it!

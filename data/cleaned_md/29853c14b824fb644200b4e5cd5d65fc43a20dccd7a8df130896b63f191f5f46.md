As of January 1, 2025, Germany has made available a new digital portal to apply for a German visa.

Applicants can choose between 28 categories of national visas online.

Hailed by Foreign Minister Annalena Baerbock as “a real administrative revolution,” it aims to ease access to necessary documents for people looking to work, study, or join their family in Germany.

“Every year, Germany is short of at least 400,000 skilled workers,” Baerbock added.

She said the “long overdue” reform is to avoid putting off talented applicants due to long paper forms and long waiting periods.

The country needs “a national visa process that is state-of-the-art – modern, digital, and secure,” she stressed.

In 2024, the German government gave out an estimated 200,000 work visas – an over 10% jump from 2023 - while the number of student visas from non-EU states rose by more than 20%.

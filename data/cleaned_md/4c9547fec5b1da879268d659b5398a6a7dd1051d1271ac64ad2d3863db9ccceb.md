> Psychological Counseling Service (en) Aktuell » Service & Counseling (en)") » Psychological Counseling Service (en)") The multi-professional counseling team of the Psychological Counseling Service of the Studierendenwerk Erlangen-Nurnberg offers psychological counseling for all fee-paying students in individual, partner and family sessions as well as in groups at **several locations** (see below). Students turn to the counseling center with a wide variety of problems. Many come because they feel affected by problems they have with themselves or others, such as difficulties with their studies or exams, contact difficulties, problems with partners or parents, sexual problems, dejection, despair, anxiety, thoughts of suicide, feelings of guilt, psychosomatic complaints, and many more. A small fee is charged for the counseling (10 Euros for individual counseling, 15 Euros for couple and family counseling), which can be waived for students in difficult financial situations. You can make an **appointment** with us or contact one of our staff members at one of our **open office hours** (even without making an appointment and giving your name) without obligation and get more information. You can find more information about making an appointment and the open office hours at our locations (see below).
> If you need help in an acute crisis situation beyond our opening hours, you can find contact information here. **Our secretary 's office can be reached by telephone at the usual times. The open counseling hours will be held as a telephone counseling until further notice. Psychological counseling can be held as telephone appointments or video counseling, as well as face-to-face appointments after prior registration and in compliance with hygiene precautions.** If you want to get in touch with us - we are here for you at these locations: Erlangen Nurnberg Eichstatt Ingolstadt Ansbach Triesdorf Our course offer: Current courses

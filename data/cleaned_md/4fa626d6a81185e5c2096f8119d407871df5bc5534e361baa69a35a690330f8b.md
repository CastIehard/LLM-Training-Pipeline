## Shape the University of the Future with Us

### Working at the University of Technology Nuremberg – Your Future, Our Vision

The UTN is a university in the making – international, interdisciplinary, sustainable and digital. We are building a modern, digitally enabled administration and offer a new work concept to shape the university of tomorrow. This approach provides researchers from all over the world with a dynamic environment for cutting-edge research and innovative teaching.

Together, we're shaping the University of the Future – become part of our team and bring your ideas to life!

At the University of Technology Nuremberg, we connect engineering and technical sciences with liberal arts and social sciences to create positive impact on our society and economy – making the UTN regionally anchored and striving for international excellence. We rely on modern teaching with digital formats, self-directed learning, and forward-thinking research. Our unique campus is currently under construction on 37 hectares in the north of Nuremberg. Once completed, it will provide space for over 6,000 students – uniting learning, teaching, and living in one place.

##### New Mobility

## Nuremberg’s Mobility in Your Hand

The NürnbergMOBIL app rethinks public transport. It integrates all means of transportation in the region into one app: public transport, the VAG_Rad and VAG Cargo Bike sharing, as well as the e-scooter provider Voi. Thanks to an individually filterable app map, your mobility becomes even more flexible and easier!

More partners will follow in the near future.!Auch das VAG_Rad in der App nutzen

##### Modern Design

## User­friendliness Meets Easy Design

The nationwide connection information and the departure monitor give you quick and easy information about public transport. They navigate you to your next destination in just a few steps.

##### Simply Fast

## Fast & Easy Ticket Purchase

With the quick purchase button, you can buy your ticket with two taps. Your active ticket is displayed immediately afterwards with just one tap on the same button! Of course, you can also buy your ticket without registration.

##### Always Up-to-Date

## Real-Time Disruption Reports and Subscriptions

Delays, construction, or a broken elevator? Our line info keeps you updated with real-time disruption information. A disruption subscription for your favorite line(s) provides you with current delays and information on days and times of your choice.

If the disruption light is green, you’re good to go!

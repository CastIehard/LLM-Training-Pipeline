# Applied Discrete Mathematics

## Prof. Dr. Christoph Hertrich

The Applied Discrete Mathematics research group combines fundamental research in discrete mathematics and theoretical computer science with applications in practically relevant fields like optimization and machine learning. A current focus of the group is to advance the mathematical understanding of artificial neural networks using methods from polyhedral geometry and combinatorial optimization.

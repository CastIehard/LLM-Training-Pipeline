# Press

We are happy to respond to inquiries from the media. Our Communication Unit team helps with the search for experts, the organization of interviews and the provision of press releases and image material. We also support the planning and implementation of media projects and answer questions about events, degree programs and research activities at our university. We look forward to your inquiry!

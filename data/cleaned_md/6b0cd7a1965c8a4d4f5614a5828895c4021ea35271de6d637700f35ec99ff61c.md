You should inform yourself about the German health insurance system.

There are two schemes: Public and private.

As an elder person (especially if you have pre-existing illnesses), private is either not an option or prohibitively expensive. So try to join the public system!

The rules for who can when join which are very complex - and advisers are only available for the private scheme. So contact a public health insurer - they are obliged to advise you (but often don't).

The public system costs a fixed percentage of your (world) income - currently around 16% (to be increased soon), subject to a minimum of approx. 280€ per month (per person).

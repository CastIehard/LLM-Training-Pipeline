> Entry/Exit System for non-EU citizens The new Entry/Exit System (EES) is introduced for non-EU citizens. The EES replaces the passport stamp and records biometric and personal data upon entry and exit. Inform about Entry/Exit System  From February 25, 2026 ETA mandatory From February 25, the United Kingdom will introduce a strict requirement for electronic travel authorization (ETA) for all visa-exempt travelers, including EU citizens. Without a valid ETA, you will be denied boarding.**Please apply for your ETA online in good time before traveling to the United Kingdom.** The authorization is digitally linked to your passport and is valid for two years.

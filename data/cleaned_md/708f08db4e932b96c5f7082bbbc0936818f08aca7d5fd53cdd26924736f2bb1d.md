# Graduate School – Your Doctorate at UTN

The Graduate School of the University of Technology Nuremberg provides a structured and supportive environment for doctoral researchers. Through targeted training opportunities, individual supervision, and diverse networking options, it supports the doctoral journey and prepares candidates for a wide range of career paths in academia, industry, and society.

Learn more

# Climate-neutral from the ground up

UTN thinks sustainably right from the start. Even before the first buildings are constructed, a well-founded climate action concept is in place – with the goal of net zero by 2040. Discover how UTN integrates climate protection into construction, operation, and teaching.

# Visitor Information

## ****Our Campus is Growing – Current Locations of UTN****

As our campus is still under development, UTN is currently spread across multiple locations. Large parts of the administration, as well as the Founding Executive Board, have already moved into the first building on campus – Cube One. The departments Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence and Liberal Arts and Social Sciences, as well as the StaRs Unit, are located in the interim buildings on Ulmenstraße and Nordostpark.

## 1\. Temporary Building on Ulmenstraße

Ulmenstraße 52i
D-90443 Nuremberg

### Arrival by public transport from Nuremberg main station

****Tramway****

Line 5 direction Worzeldorfer Straße, exit: Siemensstraße (approx. 500 m by foot)

****Subway****

U1 direction Langwasser Süd, exit: Frankenstraße (approx. 1000 m by foot)

Optional: Continue with bus line 65 direction Röthenbach, exit: Markgrafenstraße (approx. 200 m by foot)

### ****Arrival by car** **

A limited number of visitor parking spaces are available next to the main entrance. Please clarify in advance with your contact person whether a reservation is possible.

## 2\. Cube One / Campus Construction Area

Dr.-Luise-Herzberg-Straße 4
90461 Nuremberg

### ****Arrival from Nuremberg main station****

**Subway**

U1 direction Langwasser Süd, exit Bauernfeindstraße (approx. 300 m by foot)

**Car**

From the city center via Münchener Straße. Dr.-Luise-Herzberg-Straße turns off Münchener Straße to the west into the southern part of the campus of the Technical University of Nuremberg.

Please note that only a limited number of visitor parking spaces are available on the campus ground. Please ask your contact person in advance whether a reservation is possible.

Road connections to UTN. Please click on the link to follow an external link to Google Maps.

## 3\. Nordostpark

Nordostpark 93
90411 Nürnberg (Nordwestliche Außenstadt)

### ****Arrival from Nuremberg main station****

****Subway****

U2 towards the airport to Herrnhütte. There, change to bus line 30 or 31 towards Nordostpark / Am Wegfeld and travel to Nordostpark Ost (alternatively Nordostpark Mitte). From there, it is only a few minutes' walk.

******Car******

**From the city center (main train station / old town):**

Follow the signs to the airport and drive out of town via Äußere Bayreuther Straße (B2). Take the Nordostpark exit, turn onto Nordostpark and follow the signs to number 93.

****From the highway (A3):****

Take the Nuremberg-Nord / Airport exit on the A3. Take the B2 towards Nuremberg, use the Nordostpark exit, turn onto Nordostpark street and continue to No. 93.

**Parking
** The “Air Campus” parking garage operated by Schäffler Engineering GmbH (Thurn-und-Taxis-Straße 12) is located near the site and can be used free of charge.

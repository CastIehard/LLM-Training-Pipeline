# Cognitive Psychology

## Prof. Dr. Magdalena Abel

The Cognitive Psychology research group examines human learning and memory. We take a broad perspective and explore remembering at the individual level, in smaller social groups, and in large collectives. Our research explores the cognitive basis of remembering. Additionally, we are also interested in how memory operates and is used in applied settings.

**Prof. Dr. Magdalena Abel**
Professor of Cognitive Psychology

# Diversity, Equity and Inclusion

As an international and interdisciplinary university, we are committed to innovative approaches in learning, research, and teaching. We are dedicated to cultivating a culture of belonging, wellbeing, and compassion in which all members of the UTN feel respected and belong and can develop their full potential. We achieve this by actively advancing core principles like Diversity, Equity, and Inclusion (DEI) in all their dimensions.

**Ourstatement on Diversity, Equity, and Inclusion clearly reflects our commitment to these values.**

## **Strategic Steps and Activities**

Initial measures included staffing the DEI Office and participating in the "Shaping Diversity" audit of the Stifterverband für die Deutsche Wissenschaft. As part of this process, an accompanying steering group was set up to support the development and anchoring of a culture of belonging, wellbeing, and compassion at UTN. The audit is accompanied by an independent expert. In addition, a and a were elected by the founding committee. Additionally, the founding commission appointed a Representative for Equal Opportunities for Women in Science and Arts, and a Gender-Equality Officer for the Administration was designated.

The goals and measures to be implemented in the field of gender equality by 2030 are available for download in the concept document (German only).

With the statement against anti-Semitism, the UTN also explicitly expresses that anti-Semitism, discrimination and racism in any form are condemned and actively combated. The University fully endorses the “Resolution for respect at Bavarian universities and against discrimination, racism and anti-Semitism”.

> 2. Job regulations for international students in Germany
> 07.10.2024 Job regulations for international students in Germany **Informations for Students**  /fileadmin/user_upload/121220_DSW_SIK_Flyer_en.png Editor: Deutsches Studierendenwerk Service Point for Intercultural Competence Berlin 2024 Infoflyer 6 Pages **2nd, updated edition, September 2024 ** A part-time job offers international students in Germany the opportunity to gain experience, get to know Germany, make contacts and supplement the financing of their studies. The new flyer “Jobben für internationale Studierende” (“Job regulations for international students”) from the Service Point for Intercultural Competence presents residence, social and employment regulations in a concise and easily understandable manner. It thus provides an overview of the most important topics. Target group: Institutions that support international students. The flyer is available in German and English. This publication appears in print and online.

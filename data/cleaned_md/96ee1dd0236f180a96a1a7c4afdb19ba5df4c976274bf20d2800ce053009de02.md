The property tax ("Grundsteuer") differs from town to town and can depend on various factors, so you better ask the local authorities there. However, it is generally low (usually a few hundred Euros per year for any property you own).

More impotant is the property sales tax ("Grunderwerbsteuer"), which is due at the time of purchase and amounts to 6% of the purchase price in Hessen and 6,5% in Brandenburg (both have a town called Frankfurt).

Since there are also transaction and registration fees, you should budget 10% of the purchase price (plus the agent fee, if one is involved) for associated costs, on top of the purchase price.

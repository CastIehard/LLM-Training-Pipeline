# University:Future Festival 2026

Date: 23\. June 2026Time: 9:00 - 17:00Location: Ohm Innovation Center (Brucknerstraße 11, 90429 Nuremberg)

For the third time, UTN is co-hosting a partner stage at the University:Future Festival together with Technische Hochschule Nürnberg. On **June 23, 2026**, the Shifting:Stage at the Ohm Innovation Center in Nuremberg will offer exclusive insights into innovative teaching and learning approaches and present the latest research findings.

The University:Future Festival (U:FF) is the largest event dedicated to the (digital) future of academic education. As a leading conference on the digital transformation of teaching and learning, it provides orientation, networking opportunities, and inspiration—community-driven and open to all. The festival covers a broad range of topics, including AI, architectures, technology, strategy development, competencies, didactics, and much more. It is aimed at everyone engaged in the present and future of academic education: university staff and students; researchers and policymakers; companies, civil society organizations, and artists.

Tickets are available via the University:Future Festival website.

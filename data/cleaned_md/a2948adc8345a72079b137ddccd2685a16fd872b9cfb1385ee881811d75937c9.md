# German courses for Nuremberg!Deutsch lernen in Stuttgart

With the Goethe-Institut near you, you can learn German excellently. Whether you are learning German for work or for leisure, the Goethe-Institut has the right course for you in Nuremberg.
The range of German courses is diverse. For example, there are evening courses, intensive courses and online courses. You decide what is suitable for you and fits into your everyday life. We determine the language level for you in a free placement test, so that you can continue where you actually are.

### By train:

With the ICE, you can be in Munich in one hour twice an hour. There is also a regional express train from Nuremberg to Munich every two hours. You can reach the Goethe-Institut in a few minutes by S-Bahn (S1, S2, S3, S8) without changing trains.

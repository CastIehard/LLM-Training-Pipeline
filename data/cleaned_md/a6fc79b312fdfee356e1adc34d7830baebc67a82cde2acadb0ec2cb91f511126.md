# International Engagement

## Erasmus Charter: Driving International Collaboration

The University of Technology Nuremberg sees itself as an internationally oriented institution. Our goal is to integrate global perspectives into teaching, research, and study, and to actively shape international collaboration.

Our international engagement strengthens global connections, creates opportunities for students and staff, and fosters sustainable partnerships with leading institutions around the world. We work towards enabling a wide range of international experiences—whether through academic exchange, internships, or research stays—and promote active dialogue through international partnerships.

A key framework for our European activities is the Erasmus Charter for Higher Education (ECHE). It defines the quality standards for participating in Erasmus+ and forms the foundation for our mobility and cooperation projects across Europe.

We are building strategic partnerships with universities and research institutions around the world. These collaborations enable exchange programs, joint research projects, and the transfer of academic knowledge.

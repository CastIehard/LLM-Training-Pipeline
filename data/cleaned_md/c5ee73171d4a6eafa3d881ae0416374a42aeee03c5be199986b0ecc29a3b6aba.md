## A Leading Research Ecosystem in the Nuremberg Metropolitan Region

The Nuremberg Metropolitan Region is one of Germany’s strongest research hubs, home to more than 50 leading R&D institutions – including renowned Fraunhofer and Max Planck institutes as well as excellent universities.
At the same time, the region is characterized by a particular strength in knowledge transfer: a diverse innovation ecosystem comprising international corporations, high-performing small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs), institutions, and dynamic start-ups provides fertile ground for innovation. At the heart of this environment, the University of Technology Nuremberg (UTN) positions itself as a future-oriented partner for interdisciplinary research, international exchange, and the effective transfer of knowledge and technology to society and industry. Competence and technology clusters in key areas such as artificial intelligence, hydrogen technologies, and sustainable energies create optimal conditions for quickly and effectively transforming scientific ideas into real innovations.

## Perfect Location in Europe

UTN is located at the center of Europe – in a region where international collaboration is second nature. Nuremberg Airport connects you to major hubs such as Amsterdam, Zurich, or Istanbul. High-speed rail links provide direct access to Berlin, Munich, Frankfurt, or Vienna – putting you at the heart of European academic dialogue within just a few hours. Night trains and a dense public transport network ensure convenient mobility for research cooperation, conferences, and international guests.

## Urban Living, Nature Close By – Ideal for Everyday Life and Family

The Franconian Switzerland, Altmühltal Nature Park, culture and cuisine – here, recreation begins at your doorstep. Families benefit from excellent infrastructure and a high quality of life. Schools, childcare, and safe neighborhoods provide the best conditions for family life and for everyone who wants to stay.

## Living and Working in the Nuremberg Metropolitan Region – International, Safe, Enjoyable

The Nuremberg Metropolitan Region is home to around 3.6 million people, shaped by a diverse community from over 160 nations. This cultural variety enriches both academic and social life.

For international researchers and their families, the region offers ideal starting conditions: international schools and bilingual childcare make the transition smooth, while a wide range of cultural and leisure activities help you settle in quickly.

Quality of life is high – and measurable: healthcare is excellent, infrastructure is reliable, and the crime rate is below the national average. Whether for a temporary research stay or a long-term academic career – many who come here find not only a workplace, but a new home.

## Learn more about UTN – and get to know the Experience Cubes!

In the Experience Cubes on our campus, you can find out more details about the concept and the planning status of the university. The four info containers focus on UTN's four core messages: interdisciplinary, international, digital and sustainable. Each of the cubes contains a multimedia exhibit inviting you to interactively explore the respective topic. By the way: the Experience Cubes are designed for sustainable and long-term use: We will regularly update the content and thus the Cubes will grow with the University of Technology Nuremberg.

We offer guided tours for **school classes**. Appointments can be arranged individually. We look forward to your inquiry at study@utn.de.

If you have any further questions or requests regarding the Experience Cubes, please feel free to contact us at events@utn.de. We’re happy to assist you.

Visiting the Experience Cubes is **free of charge**.

## Directions to the Experience Cubes

The Experience Cubes are located on our construction site on Dr.-Luise-Herzberg-Straße in the south of Nuremberg. Access via Münchener Straße (outbound). We recommend public transportation: Take subway U1 in direction Langwasser Süd (Station: Bauernfeindstraße). You will arrive at the site after a short walk.

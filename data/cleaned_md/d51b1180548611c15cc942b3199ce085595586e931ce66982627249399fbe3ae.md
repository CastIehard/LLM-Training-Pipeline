# University Contract of UTN

The UTN was founded on January 1st, 2021 as the tenth state university in Bavaria. This is the first state university to be founded in Bavaria since 1978. On September 21st, 2023, the UTN leadership signed a university contract with the Bavarian State Ministry of Science and the Arts, represented by State Minister Markus Blume.

The UTN is a technical university under construction. The UTN university contract therefore focuses primarily on strategic goals, the achievement of which will promote the development and growth of the UTN in teaching, research, transfer, innovation and personnel development in the coming years.

The UTN university contract is based on the framework agreement of the Bavarian universities and their 10 fields of action, with the following focuses:

1. **Student experience, education, and further education:** Establishment of master's degree programs and a bachelor's degree program in a central School of Students and Young Researchers; innovative learning design as well as a variety of methods in teaching; consistent interdisciplinary integration and use of the English language in teaching; digitalized student lifecycle.
1. **Quality assurance in research, teaching and administration:** setting up a digital reporting system; establishing profile-building standards for quality assurance; using an agile strategy of iterative refinement in the design and implementation of structures and processes for quality assurance and establishing internationally competitive quality in research, teaching and administration.

The AI focus of the UTN (AI) was determined after the signing of this university contract. The UTN's goals in these fields of action are entirely consistent with this AI focus, which the UTN welcomes and whose implementation in these fields of action has already begun.

Information on the framework agreement for universities: Agility, excellence and innovation (in German) for Bavaria's higher education landscape can be found on the website (in German) of the Bavarian State Ministry of Science and the Arts.

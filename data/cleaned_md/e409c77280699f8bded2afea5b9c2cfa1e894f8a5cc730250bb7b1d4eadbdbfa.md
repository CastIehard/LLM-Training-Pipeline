# Foundations of Machine Learning

## Prof. Claire Vernade

The Foundations of Machine Learning Research Group focuses on understanding the principles that should guide the design of machine learning algorithms. An important focus of the team is around reinforcement learning and dynamical systems, which are central to modern applications of machine learning.

**Prof. Claire Vernade**
Professor of Foundations of Machine Learning

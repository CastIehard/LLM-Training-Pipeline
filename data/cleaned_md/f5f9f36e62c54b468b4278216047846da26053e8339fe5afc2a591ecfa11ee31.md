If I understand your post correctly, there is a person living in one Schengen country (with a national residence permit) and wants to visit Germany. That is possible within the Schengen rules (up to 90 days), no additional visa required.

After the residence permit expires, the person would need to apply for a visa (if one is needed for his/her nationality).

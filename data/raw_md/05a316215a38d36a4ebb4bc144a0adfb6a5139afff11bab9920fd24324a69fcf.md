# Your Start at UTN

Before arriving in Nuremberg, there are some things you should consider in preparing for your arrival as well as your studies.

## Living in Nürnberg

One of the most important tasks before starting your studies or job is to search for a suitable apartment. Since housing is in high demand, especially at the beginning of each semester, we recommend you start looking for a suitable room or apartment as early as possible, ideally as soon as you have been admitted or accepted a job at UTN.

## The Residents' Registration Office

You must register at the Residents' Registration Office within 14 days of moving into your new apartment in Nuremberg (or a nearby city).

## The Journey to Nuremberg

Nuremberg is very well connected nationally and internationally. As Albrecht Dürer Airport Nürnberg is served by major airlines several times each day, it is a good destination airport for international travelers. From the airport, you can reach Nuremberg's city center in just a few minutes by subway.

Even from Germany's largest airport, Frankfurt Airport , you can reach Nuremberg's main train station in just under two and a half hours by ICE train.

## BAföG Application

The Federal Government offers financial assistance for training and studies with the Federal Training Assistance Act (BAföG). Currently, the maximum monthly rate is 934 EUR. You can get all of the information you need about BAföG directly from the Studierendenwerk Erlangen-Nürnberg or from the BayernPortal.

If you have any questions about your BAföG application, the Amt für Ausbildungsförderung in Nuremberg will be happy to help you.

## Welcome Week for Students

One week before courses begin, a Welcome Week is held for all new national and international students. During Welcome Week, you will have an opportunity to get to know your new university, meet other students, and familiarize yourself with your surroundings.

## UTN Onboarding for Doctoral Candidates and Postdocs

Similar to the Welcome Week for students, there is an onboarding for doctoral candidates and postdocs. This consists of different stations within UTN and offers you a first look at the different areas and tasks of the university. The date and time for this event will be communicated to you either by Human Resources or the respective department.

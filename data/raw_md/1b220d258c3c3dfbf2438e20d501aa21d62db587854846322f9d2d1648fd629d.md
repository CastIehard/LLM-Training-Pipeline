#  How to apply 

        1. [Start](/en/)
        2. How to apply

Welcome to our applications page! If you would like to study in Germany and uni-assist handles your application, this is the page for you. We will guide you through the application process, step by step. You will find helpful information and tips for each of the six steps - after all, we want your application to be a success. Let's go!

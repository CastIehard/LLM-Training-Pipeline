## ****Our Central Service Units****

![](https://www.utn.de/files/2024/10/AdobeStock_Mikus-837667474.jpg)

#### UTN School of Students and Young Researchers

StaRs is more than a traditional graduate school - it organizes teaching and offers students, doctoral researchers, postdocs and teaching staff a wide range of advisory and support services.

![](https://www.utn.de/files/2024/05/AdobeStock_Drobot_Dean_118343301.jpg)

#### Teams of the Founding Executive Board

Key decisions at the university are shaped with the support of the Founding Executive Board's teams – from high-level strategy to direct assistance to the Executive Board.

![](https://www.utn.de/files/2024/02/ES-AdobeStock_tippapatt-637148965.jpg)

#### Communication Unit

From press relations to social media, events, marketing, and brand development – the Communication Unit is responsible for UTN's external presence and public image.

![](https://www.utn.de/files/2024/10/AdobeStock_ndabcreativity-840444166.jpg)

#### Human Resources and Legal Unit

Supports university staff and leaders in human resources and employment law matters and ensures that internal processes consistently meet legal requirements.

![](https://www.utn.de/files/2024/10/AdobeStock_ME-Image-637648103.jpg)

#### Real Estate Unit

The Real Estate Unit coordinates construction projects, manages technical, infrastructural and commercial facility management, as well as sustainability and occupational safety.

![](https://www.utn.de/files/2024/11/AdobeStoc-contrastwerkstatt_61026764.jpg)

#### IT Unit

The IT Unit provides modern infrastructure, drives information management, digital working and learning, and creates foundations for research and teaching – from compute resources to AI.

![](https://www.utn.de/files/2025/04/Legal-AdobeStock_Lek_730547952.jpg)

#### Finance and Procurement Unit

Responsible for financial and procurement processes across the university, with the Finance and Procurement Unit covering budget planning, purchasing, and the responsible use of funds.

![](https://www.utn.de/files/2023/08/Beitrag-Daphne-AdobeStock_SKW-565944185.jpg)

#### Department Computer Science & Artificial Intelligence

The Department of Computer Science & Artificial Intelligence combines various disciplines of computer science and robotics to develop pioneering technologies.

![Six people sit on the floor in a circle, engaging with laptops, notebooks, and a tablet.](https://www.utn.de/files/2024/12/HAI-AdobeStock_K_Abrahams_peopleimages_559977564.jpg)

#### Department Liberal Arts and Social Sciences

Historical and systematic humanities and social sciences answer pressing questions of our time in close collaboration with engineering and natural sciences.

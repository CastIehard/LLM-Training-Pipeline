# Share your experience, empower expats

Help other expats by getting involved in the Expat.com community

Share your knowledge on living abroad, support fellow expats, and make a difference in the global community: join the Experts team!

## What is an Expat.com Expert?

Moving abroad is an exciting yet challenging experience: Expat.com Experts are here to help fellow expat members navigate their expat journey.  
Experts are experienced expats who wish to get involved and contribute to the community by helping other members. Based on their experience abroad, they guide members about life in the destination they live in, sharing tips on daily life, culture, formalities or expat challenges. They foster meaningful connections and help build a supportive expat community.

## Why become an Expert?

__

##### Make a difference

Support expats settling into your destination, help them adapt and thrive

 __

##### Be recognized

Be acknowledged as a trusted voice in your destination

 __

##### Expand your network

Connect with expats from around the world

 __

##### Contribute to the global expat community

Be actively involved in a platform that unites and supports expats worldwide

## What's the role of an Expert?

Answering questions in the forum about their destination

  


Sharing advice, tips and recommendations through posts and comments

  


Sharing practical info and news about life in their host country

  


Welcoming new members to the platform

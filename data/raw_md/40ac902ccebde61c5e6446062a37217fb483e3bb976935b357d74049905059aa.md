# Study in Germany – The Podcast

German Academic Exchange Service (DAAD)

Folgen

Welcome to "Study in Germany - The Podcast", your new guide to getting ready for life in Germany. We'll be talking about all the big questions, from "How to find the right study programme?" to "How to find a place to live?" and "How to start your career?" to the everyday stuff like "Why is everybody in love with drug stores in Germany?" and "Do they really eat this much bread?" Our host Jana talks to real experts: Students from all over the world who decided to study in Germany - and sometimes even to stay for good. read less

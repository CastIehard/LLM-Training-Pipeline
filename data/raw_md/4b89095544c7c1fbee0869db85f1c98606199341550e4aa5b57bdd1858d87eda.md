##  Internationalization and intercultural opening 

International students, applicants and interested parties will find a lot of useful information about studying in Germany on our portal [www.internationale-studierende.de](http://www.internationale-studierende.de/). Here we answer questions that international students often ask. The pages are structured according to the phases of study preparation, arrival in Germany, course of studies and graduation.

##  Other intercultural topics 

###  Service Point for Intercultural Competence 

Establishing a welcoming culture for international students. The Service Center for Intercultural Competence (SIK) shows how this can be done with a broad information, consulting and continuing education program.

[Read more...](/en/themen/internationale-studierende/servicepoint-for-intercultural-competence)

###  Service for international students 

The 57 Studierendenwerke in Germany ensure that students are offered more than just a research and teaching program at their university, and that they feel comfortable and welcome here. They shape the general conditions for studying and cater to the diversity of students.

[Read more...](/en/topics/international-students/services-for-international-students)

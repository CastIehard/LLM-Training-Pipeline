Hi everyone! 😊

  


I’m Paola, originally from Guatemala and I’ve been living in Germany for 13 years. I live near Nürnberg with my three children.

  


I love dancing, meeting new people and discovering different cultures – that’s always been my favorite way to feel at home wherever I go.

  


And speaking of feeling at home... I’m also a Finanzcoach (financial advisor) helping expats from all over the world make sense of the German system – things like health insurance, savings, pensions and financial planning.

  


I know how confusing it all can be when you're starting out (or even after years here!), so I enjoy supporting others with clear, friendly guidance.

  


Happy to connect, chat, share experiences – or dance a little if the opportunity shows up! 💃✨

 __15612

immoscout24.de gives an estimate of the indirect costs of purchase with every property they list.

These typically include property purchase tax (in Berlin 6% of the purchase price), notarization and registration fees (approx. 1.5%) and agent fee (if one is involved, typically 3.5 - 4.6%).

However, a note of caution: Restrictions, fees, buerocracy (all in German) and the difficulty buying/selling property make it a BAD short-term investment. Unless you have been here several years AND plan on staying at least 10 years, I would NOT advise you to buy any property!

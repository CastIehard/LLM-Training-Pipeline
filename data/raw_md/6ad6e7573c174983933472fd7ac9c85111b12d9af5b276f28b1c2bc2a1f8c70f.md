##  Applying for a place in a hall of residence 

**Places in Studierendenwerke halls of residence are very popular – and run out very quickly. You are recommended to contact the study location's Studierendenwerk well in advance of the start of the semester.**

[ ![Blick in den Eingang eines Wohnheims mit Briefkästen.](/fileadmin/user_upload/Stockbilder/Contentbilder/WohnenFReiburg_KHerschelmann_04.jpg) The early bird catches the worm: apply early for places in halls of residence | (c) Kay Herschelmann ](/fileadmin/user_upload/Stockbilder/Contentbilder/WohnenFReiburg_KHerschelmann_04.jpg)

## How can I apply for a place in a hall of residence?

The local _  Studierendenwerke _have detailed information about application procedures. There are numerous accommodation services on _Studierendenwerke  _websites, including online forms, information on halls of residence, photos and floor plans.

## Allocation criteria

Allocation criteria differ depending on the location. Some _Studierendenwerke  _allocate places using lots, others award places in chronological order based on the dates applications are received.

Special attention is given to certain groups of people when allocating places in halls of residence, including:

        * students with physical disabilities or chronic illnesses,
        * students with extraordinarily difficult personal circumstances,
        * international students are usually allocated a room as part of a service package offered by 40 _Studierendenwerke_.

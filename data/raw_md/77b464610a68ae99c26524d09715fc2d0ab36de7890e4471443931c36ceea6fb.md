@adharpawar  
  
Following are the answers for your questions:  
  
1\. As u said you have already started the process, Which type of service you have applied for ? whether JOb seeker visa or any thing else in which they will try for your JOb offer first, hence you will be secure on EU blue card ?  
  
A. Easy Start Consulting process (try to get the job offer first and then apply for EU Blue card)  
  
2\. How much they cost for it?   
  
A. They would charge you 499 Euros max.  
  
3\. How Is German language ? Is it hard to learn ?  
  
German language sounds interesting but tough though. I'm sure it could be done if you have passion in learning language and it depends on how you learn it. I kicked off with in-house training at our company but it has been suspended due to lack of time. Though classes were suspended, i always had passion to continue my learning. Sometimes i do self paced learning session and sometimes i don't. I'm planning to write A1 exam in the next 6 months after my training at Goethe Institut, Delhi. Since i'm a working professional, i have registered for the blended learning course for 6 months before i could take up an exam. Just Google Goethe and contact your nearest center for further information. Once you learn the language it is essential to get it certified from German Consulate / Goethe. It might help you in future.  
  
4\. Could you please share your contact and other info or links on German work permit visa on my mail id :   
  
EU blue card is the only permanent work permit that is available as far as my knowledge is concerned. Job seeker visa is meant only for freshers/recent pass out/university grads.  
  
You may reach me at aspirevenman@gmail.com  
  
IT and Automotive industries in Germany need highly skilled professionals. I'm from automotive simulation background. I'm hoping for the best. Good luck for your future.

Hello,

  


I hope you are doing well.

  


I am a German citizen currently residing in Vietnam and I hold a German driving license issued in Berlin. I would like to better understand my options regarding a few driving-license related matters.

  


First, I would like to know whether it is possible to apply for an official International Driving Permit (IDP) while living abroad. If applying from abroad is not possible, I would like to know whether a third party could apply and collect it on my behalf with a power of attorney, or whether I could apply for it during a short visit to Germany even though I am no longer officially registered there.

  


Second, I am interested in obtaining the B196 extension, which allows car license holders to ride motorcycles up to 125cc after completing the required training. I would like to know whether it would be possible to complete the training and obtain this extension during a temporary stay in Germany without being officially registered there.

  


Finally, if these options are not possible in Germany, I would also appreciate guidance on whether there are countries where a motorcycle license can be obtained as a tourist that would later be recognized or usable within the EU and that has long validity.

  


Any information or guidance would be greatly appreciated.

  


Thank you very much for your time and assistance.

**@Gold212**

An International Driving permit is nothing more than a multilanguage translation of one's home license. The ADAC website shows a German license holder will indeed need an international premit there. If so, it can usually be obtained in the local country. This depends on how things are done in Vietnam. Otherwise, you suggest a scenario of being back in Germany on a tourist trip. If so, it takes 15 minutes and 15 Euros at the ADAC office to get the International License if you can show your valid German one. Not positive, but think any additional licensing like a permit to conduct motorcycles is not likely allowed without being registered in Germany despite being a citizen.

# Your essential partner to make your move a breeze

Expat.com is the leading platform dedicated to life abroad. With over 3 million members, it provides free information, advice, networking opportunities and services. It is an essential tool for a successful expat journey.

## The story behind Expat.com

In 2005, [Julien Faliu](/en/profile/2-julien.html), freshly graduated, decided to live abroad. He soon realized that, at that time, very little information was available about expat life. He decided to create a platform to gather blogs from expats worldwide, a unique source of firsthand insights into life abroad. He dedicated his free time to manage this space aimed at sharing experiences and which evolved little by little into a comprehensive resource to support you through every stage of your expat journey.

## What Expat.com offers you

On Expat.com, you will find:   

        * [Guides](/en/guide/), covering the main expat concerns: visas, housing, healthcare, jobs and more.
        * [Forums](/en/forum/) to share experiences and insights with [other expats](/en/network/)
        * Practical tools for your daily life: [business directory](/en/business/), [classifieds](/en/classifieds/), [property](/en/housing/) and [jobs](/en/jobs/) listings, [services](/en/services/) tailored to expats' needs
        * A [magazine](/en/expat-mag/) to explore the latest news and trends of global mobility
        * An [events](/en/events/) calendar
        * A [photo gallery](/en/pictures/) with glimpses into life abroad.
  
Whether you're planning to move or already living abroad, you will find everything you need to make your expat journey smoother.

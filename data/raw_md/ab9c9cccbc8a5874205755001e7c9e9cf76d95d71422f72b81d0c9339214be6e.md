#  Master 

If you would like to begin a [Master's course](/en/tools/glossary-of-terms/description/term/masters-course/ "Postgraduate studies undertaken after completed undergraduate studies.") after your [undergraduate studies](/en/tools/glossary-of-terms/description/term/undergraduate-studies/ "All courses of study leading up to the first university degree."), this is what you need to know about [admission criteria](/en/tools/glossary-of-terms/description/term/admission-criteria/ "Criteria that need to be fulfilled in order to be admitted to a course.").

        * [Criteria check ](/en/how-to-apply/get-information/master/#c1752)
        * [Getting information](/en/how-to-apply/get-information/master/#c1763)

* * *

##  Criteria check 

#### Completed undergraduate studies

In order to apply for a [Master's course](/en/tools/glossary-of-terms/description/term/masters-course/ "Postgraduate studies undertaken after completed undergraduate studies.") in Germany, you generally need to have completed [undergraduate studies](/en/tools/glossary-of-terms/description/term/undergraduate-studies/ "All courses of study leading up to the first university degree.") in a related subject. At some universities you can already apply before completing your first degree.

Your degree must be equivalent to a German [bachelor](/en/tools/glossary-of-terms/description/term/bachelor/ "The standard degree awarded after undergraduate studies at a university.") degree. This involves the following criteria, among others: The initial degree must be attained at a university which is recognised ([accredited](/en/tools/glossary-of-terms/description/term/accreditation/ "A process which ensures the quality of courses at a university.")) according to the laws of the country in question. Some countries also require the course of study itself to be [accredited](/en/tools/glossary-of-terms/description/term/accreditation/ "A process which ensures the quality of courses at a university.").

######  Checking recognition 

You can check the recognition of your university using the information portal "[anabin](/en/tools/glossary-of-terms/description/term/anabin/ "A database you can use to find out how your certificate will be evaluated in Germany.")" of the [Central Office for Foreign Education (ZAB)](/en/tools/glossary-of-terms/description/term/zab-central-office-for-foreign-education/ "A central institution for the assessment of non-German educational certificates in Germany."). This is also where you can find out if your degree is equivalent to a German [bachelor](/en/tools/glossary-of-terms/description/term/bachelor/ "The standard degree awarded after undergraduate studies at a university.") degree.

[anabin](http://anabin.kmk.org)

####  Your result - what happens next? 

        * Equivalent degree
        * Not sure
        * German degree

### Equivalent degree

If you have attained a degree at a university outside of Germany which is equivalent to a German [bachelor](/en/tools/glossary-of-terms/description/term/bachelor/ "The standard degree awarded after undergraduate studies at a university.") degree, please read our guidelines on additional criteria.

### Not sure

Are you unsure about the recognition of your first degree in Germany? We will find out for you when processing your application. You should find out about additional criteria now.

### German degree

Have you completed your [bachelor](/en/tools/glossary-of-terms/description/term/bachelor/ "The standard degree awarded after undergraduate studies at a university.") studies in Germany? For some universities, uni-assist is responsible for evaluating your applications in this case as well. You should find out about additional criteria now.

* * *

####  Language proficiency 

In Germany, many master's courses are taught in German. Therefore, you generally need to show adequate [proficiency in German](/en/how-to-apply/assemble-your-documents/language-certificates/).

Some courses are offered in English. In that case, you need little or no proficiency in German. You can find an overview of international university courses using the [DAAD](/en/tools/glossary-of-terms/description/term/daad/ "An organization which assists the international exchange of students and scientists.") database "[International Programmes](https://www.daad.de/deutschland/studienangebote/international-programs/en/)".

**Tip:** If your German language proficiency is not yet sufficient, many universities offer a preparatory [German language course](/en/how-to-apply/get-information/preparatory-german-language-course/). For more information, please contact the university of your choice.

* * *

####  Further criteria 

In addition to the required degree and German language proficiency criteria, there are further criteria for master's courses. These [admission criteria](/en/tools/glossary-of-terms/description/term/admission-criteria/ "Criteria that need to be fulfilled in order to be admitted to a course.") are set by the individual universities.

The criteria may include internship experience, grades and formal application requirements such as [deadlines](/en/how-to-apply/plan-your-application/deadlines-processing-time/) and [translations](/en/how-to-apply/assemble-your-documents/certified-copies-and-translations/) of documents.

* * *

##  Getting information 

To ensure a successful application, get information from the uni-assist universities** ** about your options and criteria for your application.

####  Get information on the following from the universities of your choice: 

## Criteria for your undergraduate degree

Depending on the course of study you are interested in, your initial degree course will need to be equivalent to a German [bachelor](/en/tools/glossary-of-terms/description/term/bachelor/ "The standard degree awarded after undergraduate studies at a university.") course with a duration of 3, 3.5 or 4 years. You can find out on [anabin](http://anabin.kmk.org/no_cache/filter/hochschulabschluesse.html) which German degree is equivalent to your undergraduate degree.

## Admission criteria

The required level of language proficiency, as well as other [admission criteria](/en/tools/glossary-of-terms/description/term/admission-criteria/ "Criteria that need to be fulfilled in order to be admitted to a course."), may vary between courses of study. Some master's courses require a minimum grade.

## Applying before completion of a bachelor degree

Not all universities accept applications with preliminary [educational certificates](/en/tools/glossary-of-terms/description/term/educational-certificates/ "Here: A collective term for certificates from schools and universities."). Some only do so if the [bachelor](/en/tools/glossary-of-terms/description/term/bachelor/ "The standard degree awarded after undergraduate studies at a university.") course took place in Germany or an EU country. If accepted, you will need an attestation from your university on your future degree and the studies completed so far.

## Deadlines

[application deadlines](/en/tools/glossary-of-terms/description/term/application-deadline/ "The last date on which an application is possible.") for master's courses are often set earlier than those for [bachelor](/en/tools/glossary-of-terms/description/term/bachelor/ "The standard degree awarded after undergraduate studies at a university.") courses. You can find more information under [Deadlines and processing time](/en/how-to-apply/plan-your-application/deadlines-processing-time/).

## Who handles applications?

uni-assist is not automatically responsible for all applications to a uni-assist university.

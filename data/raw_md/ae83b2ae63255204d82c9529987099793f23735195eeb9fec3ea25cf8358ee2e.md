 __2795

Never dealt with such a thing but Googled the form V0901 (link below). It needs to be filed with the Deutsche Rentenversicherung (German Retirement Insurance office). They are obviously the ones to contact with any such question. 

  


The form is wirtten in both German and English. Section 13 states that any documentation needs to be submitted in its original for OR be certified/notarized by the issueing agency in the corressponding country. Thus an India issued document needs to be notarized in India and a German issued one by the German issuing agent. 

  


As far as the signature, it doesn't mention requiring a witness or notarization of any kind. Maybe best to ask them directly if in doubt.

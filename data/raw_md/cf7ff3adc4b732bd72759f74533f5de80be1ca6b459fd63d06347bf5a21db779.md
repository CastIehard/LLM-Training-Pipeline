 __15610

The thread is now in the Germany forum.

German citizenship by descent is, under normal circumstances, possible only fo kids of German nationals - were your parents German?

The only exeption I know of is people, whose German citizenship was cancelled due to discrimination (e.g. by the Nazis). If your ancestors renounced their German citizenship voluntarily (or took on another citizenship, which automatically leads to loss of the German one), you are almost certainly not German.

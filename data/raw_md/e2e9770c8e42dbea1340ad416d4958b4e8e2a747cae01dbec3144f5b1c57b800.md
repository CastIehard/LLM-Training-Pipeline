> ![](https://www.utn.de/files/2023/03/utn-study-enrollment-header-c-AdobeStock-shurkin_son_573477364.jpg) Enrollment You have been admitted to the program and now want to enroll? Then you've come to the right place!  
> Upon successful admission to the degree program and your binding acceptance via UTNexus you can start with the enrollment process OR: you can enroll! Enrollment process Once you have accepted your study place offer on your UTNexus account, you can begin the enrollment process. Please use the “Supplement Data” button to upload all documents required for enrollment. After completion, you will be able to download the enrollment application form. Please sign it by hand or digitally and upload it in your UTNexus account as well Please note that in addition to the documents, the semester fee and your health insurance status must also be successfully submitted to UTN. Without these, you cannot enroll and will lose your study place. Instructions on how to transfer your semester fee and submit your insurance confimation can be found below. **Please upload these documents to UTNexus:** **ID or passport**  
>  **Visa / Residence permit (if necessary)** – please note: you can finish enrollment without the visa/residence permit. However, all students must arrive at UTN in person by 31 October at the latest. If you require a visa/residence permit you must therefore submit it by this date.  
>  **Passport photo**  
>  **Your educational certificates (university entrance qualification, final degree certificate, transcript of records) as authentic documents** Expand All What are authentic documents?  All **educational credentials, certificates, diplomas** , and any other required documents must be uploaded as authentic documents. These must either bear the signature and seal of the issuing official authority or, alternatively, include a digital seal, a verification code, or a QR code for online verification. Websites used for online verification must be accessible in German or English.  
>  If your graduation documents are not issued in German or English, you must additionally provide a translation.  
>  Please upload only one PDF file per document. This PDF file must contain:  
>  • the document in the original language, and  
>  • the **translation** (German or English) prepared by a sworn official translator.  
>  The translation must bear the **seal and signature of the sworn translator**. Are there special regulations for certain countries?  Special requirements apply to the following countries:  
>  **China:** Graduation documents issued by Chinese schools or universities must bear both the **red round seal and the embossed round seal** of the university or notary in order to be recognized as authentic documents.  
>  **Georgia:** Graduation documents for enrollment must be uploaded as notarized copies **with an apostille**. The apostille must be affixed to both the original Georgian-language document and the translation.  
>  **Iran:** Graduation documents must be submitted in **legalized form** for enrollment. Information on legalization can be found on the [website of the German Embassy in Tehran.](https://teheran.diplo.de/ir-de/2402396-2402396?isLocal=false&isPreview=false) Do I need to upload any additional documents for enrollment besides my graduation documents?  Only educational credentials are required for enrollment, such as school certificates, transcripts, or examination results. Please do not upload CVs, letters of motivation, or language certificates for enrollment. What should I do if I have not yet received my final Bachelor’s graduation documents?  If you wish to enroll in a Master’s program but have not yet received your final Bachelor’s graduation documents, you may instead upload your final transcript of records and a certificate confirming your graduation in UTNexus. **The final degree certificates must be submitted when you register your Master’s thesis at the latest.**  
>  The certificate must confirm that you have successfully completed your degree and that the academic title has been awarded to you. It must be issued by the responsible authority, such as the examination board. The final transcript of records must list all examinations taken as well as the overall grade.  
>  The authenticity requirements described above also apply in this case.  
>  **Please note:** A hope certificate stating that you are expected to pass your exams and graduate is not sufficient for enrollment. The Bachelor’s thesis must have been graded. **As a general rule:** If there are justified doubts about the authenticity of the documents, we will contact you by email and request that you send certified copies by post or present the original documents in person.  
>  **Please note:** A certified copy is a copy of an original document that has been confirmed as authentic by an official person, authority or institution (such as a notary public, a university, or a government office). Print-outs of certified copies **cannot** be accepted for enrollment. Transfer semester fee The semester fee is **72 euros** and must be paid before your initial enrollment as well as for each subsequent semester. You can find the payment details in your UTNexus account. Please note that processing your payment can take around 1 - 2 weeks. Make sure to make the payment in time to meet the enrollment deadline (30 September for the winter semester).  Please make sure to keep proof of your payment. Health insurance All students are required to have [health insurance](https://www.study-in-germany.de/en/plan-your-studies/requirements/health-insurance/) in Germany. You cannot be enrolled without health insurance. Enrollment applications received before the insurance company has transmitted confirmation of your insurance status will be processed once received. UTN’s official **university number** is **H0003881** , in case you need to reference it. You need to apply for this transfer with your health insurance company directly. Private health insurance Do you have private health insurance? You still need to apply for transfer of your insurance status with a statutory health insurance company of your choice. In order to do so, you need to send your proof of private health insurance to a statutory health insurance company. Foreign health insurance **EU or country with social security agreement:** Present your EHIC (European Health Insurance Card) or PEB (provisional replacement for EHIC) to a German statutory health insurance. Then the transfer of your insurance status to UTN will proceed. **Non-EU country or country without social security agreement.** The statutory health insurances can verify and possibly accept your foreign insurance. In case the insurance can not be accepted, you must also get insured by a German, statutory health insurance company. **You will find a list of the statutory health insurance companies on the website[krankenkassen.de.](https://www.krankenkassen.de/gesetzliche-krankenkassen/krankenkassen-liste/ "https://www.krankenkassen.de/")** © AdobeStock/Shurkinson Archives

> Scholarship Deutschlandstipendium We are excited to announce that the University of Technology Nuremberg has received funding to offer one scholarship for a Master student. In the future, we may have even more scholarships. This scholarship aims to award highly talented students. Applicants for a Deutschlandstipendium at UTN must have a relevant **grade point average of 2.0 or better** in order to be considered for the scholarship.  Formal regulations of the Deutschlandstipendium at UTN: [Download PDF (German only)](https://www.utn.de/files/2025/06/250602_Richtlinie-Deutschlandstipendium_L0.pdf) Please also note the [information on data protection](https://www.utn.de/files/2026/03/20260303_Datenschutzhinweis_Deutschlandstipendium1.pdf) (German only). Key Details **Scholarship Type**|  Scholarship for one year with option of extension for one more year  
> ---|---  
> **Target Group**|  Enrolled Master’s students at UTN  
> **Eligibility Requirements**|  Grade point average of 2.0 or better and enrollment in a regular study program at UTN for the next two semesters (Summer 2026 and Winter 2026/27).  
> **Scholarship Amount**|  300 EUR per month (for total of 12 months)  
> **Scholarship Period**|  Summer semester 2026 to end of Winter semester 2026/2027 (April 1, 2026 – March 30, 2027)  
> **Number of Scholarships Available**|  1  
> **Application Period**|  Until February 24, 2026, 11:59 p.m. CET  
> Application Process Supporting Documents Please submit the supporting documents by the application deadline to [stars-career@utn.de](mailto:stars-career@utn.de).
>             * **Motivation letter** for the scholarship (maximum 1 page) in English.
>             * **Curriculum vitae** (CV), table-format, in English.
>             * **Certificate of enrollment** or a copy of the letter of admission, if a certificate of enrollment is not yet available.
>             * **Copy of the certificate of university entrance qualification** (Note: Applicants from non-German grading systems must have their grades converted to the German grading system. You can use the [grade conversion tool](https://www.tum.de/en/studies/application/application-info-portal/grade-conversion-formula-for-grades-earned-outside-germany) provided by TU Munich).
>             * If applicable: Current transcript of records with average grade and achieved ECTS credits.
>             * If applicable: Copies of certificates of vocational training.
>             * If applicable: Copy of the degree certificate of an already completed degree program (Bachelor’s, Master’s, Diploma, etc.).
>             * If applicable: Copies of certificates of voluntary internships.
>             * If applicable: Copies of other supporting documents (awards, certificates, prizes, employment references, etc.) that substantiate the information provided in the application form.
> Evaluation Criteria  Each application will receive between 0 and 8 points based on the following aspects:
>             * Grades of previous degrees
>             * Special achievements, awards and prizes, previous professional experience and internships
>             * Extracurricular engagement
>             * Special personal or family circumstances
> Selection Process Applications will be reviewed, and shortlisted candidates may be invited for an interview. Successful applicants will be notified via email or telephone in late February.

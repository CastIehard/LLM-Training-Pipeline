Otherwise, the navigation may be incorrect and direct you to the wooded area north of the airport. This issue has already been reported and is being addressed. Secure yourself a parking space in advance![Book your parking space now](https://parking.airport-nuernberg.de/?lang=en) ____By public transportUndergroundThe U2 underground line connects Nuremberg Central Station directly to the airport. You can buy your desired ticket online via the [VGN online store](https://shop.vgn.de/index.php/standardProductSale).__By cab Cabs are available for you around the clock at Nuremberg Airport.Taxizentrale Nürnberg __Phone[+49 911-1 94 10 ](tel:+4991119410 )__Opening hours 24/7 __Website[Website (German)](https://www.taxi-nuernberg.de/)__By bicycle Bicycle racks are available along the terminal. You can leave your bike there during your vacation.

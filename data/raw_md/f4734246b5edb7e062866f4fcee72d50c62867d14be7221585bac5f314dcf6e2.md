##  Apply in 6 steps 

Hint: With arrow keys you can switch to other slides.

![](/fileadmin/_processed_/6/7/csm_1_info_f1f1ddbbab.jpg)

### 1\. **Get information in Advance**

If you would like to study in Germany and uni-assist handles your application, this is the page for you. If you are interested in a Bachelor or Master’s course, you can find out about the requirements here. Then, ask the university of your choice about the application criteria for your chosen course of study.

[Find out more](/en/how-to-apply/get-information/ "Get information in advance")

![](/fileadmin/_processed_/b/f/csm_2_plan_1aae689c61.jpg)

### 2\. **Plan your application**

Check how to apply via uni-assist. Plan your application using our checklists and notes on deadlines. In some cases, special rules may apply, for example if you need a preliminary review documentation (VPD).

[Find out more](/en/how-to-apply/plan-your-application/ "Plan your application")

![](/fileadmin/_processed_/8/5/csm_3_collect_5d6c019b6d.jpg)

### **3\. Assemble your documents**

For all the documents required, get copies and certifications as necessary. Check whether a translation is required and, if necessary, have one made by a court-certified translator.

[Find out more](/en/how-to-apply/assemble-your-documents/ "Assemble your documents")

![](/fileadmin/_processed_/d/8/csm_4_online_d5f19bef8e.jpg)

### 4\. **Apply online**

Register for the online portal My assist. Create an online application for each chosen course. Upload documents for your application(s).

[Find out more](/en/how-to-apply/apply-online/ "Apply online")

![](/fileadmin/_processed_/e/3/csm_5_pay_d9e6739bfb.jpg)

### 5\. **Pay All fees**

Pay the handling fees for all your applications. Once we have received your payment and your documents, we can start processing your application.

[Find out more](/en/how-to-apply/pay-all-fees/ "Paying for fees")

![](/fileadmin/_processed_/e/d/csm_6_send_3cfd5a38e6.jpg)

### 6\. **Send & Track**

Send us all required documents by letter. Track what is happening at uni-assist and at the universities.

[Find out more](/en/how-to-apply/send-track/ "Send and Track")

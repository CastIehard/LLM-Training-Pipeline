I did not do that myself, but I understand that in commerial leases there exists no tenant protection (similar to what we have fo residential rentals in Germany). Thus you really need to know what you are doing BEFORE signing a lease - if you are not firm in this (AND fluent in legalese German), the expense of engaging a lawyer can be a good investment!

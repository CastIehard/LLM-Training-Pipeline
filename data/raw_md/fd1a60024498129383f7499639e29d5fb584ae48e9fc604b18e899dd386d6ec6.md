>             * Our Network in Germany The German EURAXESS Network currently is composed of over 100 EURAXESS Centres located at universities and research institutions across the country. They offer professional counselling for visiting researchers at their institutions.Information about German members of the EURAXESS Network, our bi-annual conferences and our projects can be found on our Network EURAXESS Germany pages. [Network EURAXESS Germany](https://www.euraxess.de/germany/network-euraxess-germany) ![Leaves representing networks](https://www.euraxess.de/sites/default/files/styles/euraxess_m_991_/public/2024-01/IMG_4996.JPG.webp?itok=472SJfzt) 79 German EURAXESS Centres are members of the European EURAXESS Network

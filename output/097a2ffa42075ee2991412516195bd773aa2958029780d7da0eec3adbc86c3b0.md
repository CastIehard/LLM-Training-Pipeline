# Scholarship

## Deutschlandstipendium

We are excited to announce that the University of Technology Nuremberg has received funding to offer one scholarship for a Master student. In the future, we may have even more scholarships. This scholarship aims to award highly talented students.

Applicants for a Deutschlandstipendium at UTN must have a relevant **grade point average of 2.0 or better** in order to be considered for the scholarship.

Formal regulations of the Deutschlandstipendium at UTN: Download PDF (German only)

### Key Details

**Scholarship Type**| Scholarship for one year with option of extension for one more year
---|---
**Target Group**| Enrolled Master’s students at UTN
**Eligibility Requirements**| Grade point average of 2.0 or better and enrollment in a regular study program at UTN for the next two semesters (Summer 2026 and Winter 2026/27)
**Scholarship Amount**| 300 EUR per month (for total of 12 months)
**Scholarship Period**| Summer semester 2026 to end of Winter semester 2026/2027 (April 1, 2026 – March 30, 2027)
**Number of Scholarships Available**| 1
**Application Period**| Until February 24, 2026, 11:59 p.m. CET

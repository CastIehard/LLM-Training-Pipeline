**Welcome to the university catering service of the Erlangen-Nuremberg Student Union!**

We would like to provide you with comprehensive information about the Student Union's catering services. For us, eating is more than just consuming food - it is enjoyment, cultural heritage, socialising and responsibility for the environment all rolled into one:

At the Studierendenwerk, we are fully committed to ensuring the optimal quality and origin of all products, which are freshly prepared for you every day by our more than 200 employees. Our canteens and cafeterias are a central place for social interaction and an integral part of student life. Environmental protection, animal welfare and sustainability play a key role for us: we can all contribute greatly to protecting our environment through our eating habits. Accordingly, we offer our guests a diverse selection, label particularly climate-friendly dishes and are committed to adhering to high animal welfare standards when purchasing.

We are always open to questions and suggestions and look forward to hearing from our guests - this has already resulted in many great projects!

The entire university catering team wishes you bon appetit and looks forward to your visit.

Do you have any questions, requests or suggestions? Write to us!

We currently operate 28 university catering facilities such as canteens, cafeterias and cafe bars at six university locations in the region.

In addition to the current menus for all canteens, these pages also provide information on cashless payment with our canteen chip card and on closing times (the partially changed opening hours of our canteens, cafeterias and cafe bars during the semester holidays or over Christmas and New Year).

Information about how we work and the awards we have received in recent years is also available in this section.

In addition to its catering facilities, the Erlangen-Nuremberg Student Services Association also operates an event service.
